The difference

Classroom in Hong Kong. Practice in Zhongshan.

This route integrates rigorous academic training at PolyU's Hong Kong campus with immersive industry experience at the Zhongshan Research Institute, located within the comprehensive Cuiheng Group industrial ecosystem.

You learn how a therapy is developed and commercialised — then step inside an operating innovation cluster and watch those decisions being made in real time.

Curriculum

What you'll study

The same four pillars as the flagship route — applied against a live industry backdrop.

🧪

Biopharmaceutical science

Proficiency in the modalities shaping modern medicine, from biologics through to cell and gene therapies.

🔐

Patenting & IP strategy

How intellectual property is built, defended and licensed — and why it decides an asset's commercial fate.

⚖️

Regulation & clinical trials

Regulatory knowledge covering trial design, approval pathways and compliance obligations.

📈

Market entry

Commercial insight into positioning, pricing and launching a therapy into a competitive market.

Why the Greater Bay Area

Where the manufacturing actually happens

The GBA has become one of the densest biopharmaceutical development and manufacturing corridors in the world. Studying beside it — rather than reading about it — changes what you understand.

  • Applied experience inside an operating industrial ecosystem.
  • Professional network across mainland and Hong Kong industry.
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration with practising scientists and commercial teams.
  • Career signal — a placement on your CV, not just coursework.

Where graduates go

The internship route suits those aiming at operational and commercial roles in the region:
