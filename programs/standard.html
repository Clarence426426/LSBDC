Curriculum

Four pillars, one commercial mindset

The curriculum integrates the scientific, business, regulatory and ethical aspects of the biopharmaceutical landscape.

🧬

Advanced therapeutics

Biologics and protein engineering through to cell and gene therapies — how today's most complex medicines are designed and produced.

⚖️

Regulation & approval

Clinical trial design and the drug approval process: what regulators expect, and how evidence is built to satisfy them.

🔐

Patents & IP strategy

Patenting strategies that protect an asset, define freedom to operate and underpin every licensing conversation.

📈

Market entry & pricing

Commercial acumen — positioning, pricing and the route a therapy takes from approval to real patients.

Learning outcomes

What you'll be able to do

Graduates leave fluent in both halves of the industry conversation — able to hold their own with a research team in the morning and a commercial board in the afternoon.

  • Scientific proficiency in cutting-edge therapeutic modalities.
  • Commercial acumen covering market entry and pricing decisions.
  • Regulatory expertise spanning trials, approval and compliance.
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration across science, law and business teams.

Where graduates go

The skill set maps directly onto the roles biopharma companies struggle to fill:
