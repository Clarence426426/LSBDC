How it works

One year in Hong Kong. One year in Singapore.

You complete the biopharmaceutical core at PolyU, then move to Nanyang Technological University to build the data science half of the qualification.

Year 1

The Hong Kong Polytechnic University

Hung Hom, Hong Kong

The biopharmaceutical foundation: biologics and protein engineering, cell and gene therapies, clinical trials, drug approval, patenting strategy and market entry — the scientific, legal and financial complexities of drug commercialization.

Year 2

Nanyang Technological University

Singapore

The MSc in Biomedical Data Science at NTU: the analytical and computational methods that now drive target discovery, trial design, real-world evidence and commercial decision-making across the sector.

Admission

Entry requirements

A Bachelor's degree or the equivalent in biological/life sciences, chemistry, biomedical sciences/engineering, health science, or a related discipline.

  • Preference will be given to applicants having at least 1 year of relevant work experience.
  • If you are not a native speaker of English, and your Bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification is awarded by institutions where the medium of instruction is not English, you are expected to provide one of the following proficiency test results (taken in a single sitting within 2 years) to fulfil the University's minimum English language requirement for admission purposes:
    • An overall score of 80 out of 120 / 4.5 out of 6 (for tests taken on or after 21 Jan 2026) or above in the Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL) Internet-based test
    • OR
    • An Overall Band score of 6.0 or above in the International English Language Testing System (IELTS) Academic module

    Remarks: TOEFL iBT Home Edition, the IELTS Online, IELTS Indicator test, and IELTS One Skill Retake results will not be considered for meeting the English language requirement for taught programmes.

Please refer to the Admission Requirements section for details.

Tuition

Costed across two institutions

Because the degree spans two universities, tuition is charged in two parts — the PolyU component in Hong Kong dollars and the NTU component in Singapore dollars.

Living costs, travel between Hong Kong and Singapore, and visa fees are not included.

Why the combination

Biopharma runs on data now

Development decisions that were once made on intuition are made on evidence — and the people who can read both the biology and the dataset are rare.

  • Two credentials from two internationally recognised universities.
  • Two markets — a professional network in Hong Kong and in Singapore.
  • Scarce skill pairing of therapeutic development with data science.
  • Regional mobility across two of Asia's leading biotech hubs.
