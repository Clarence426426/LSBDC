You approach a PolyU professor to supervise you, then spend six months on a hands-on research project in their group.

If you're aiming at a PhD Gives you genuine research experience to draw on in future applications. It is research training only — not a PhD placement, and no commitment from the supervisor beyond the project.

Why this degree

Science alone doesn't reach patients. Strategy does.

The curriculum integrates the scientific, business, regulatory and ethical dimensions of the biopharmaceutical landscape — so you can move a therapy from discovery all the way to approval and launch.

🧬

Cutting-edge therapeutics

Biologics, protein engineering, and cell & gene therapies — the modalities defining the next decade of medicine.

⚖️

Regulatory expertise

Clinical trials, drug approval pathways and the intellectual property strategy that protects an asset's value.

📈

Commercial acumen

Market entry, pricing and business development — how a therapy becomes a viable, fundable product.

🤝

Interdisciplinary teams

Work the way industry works: scientists, regulators, analysts and commercial leads solving problems together.

🌏

Three geographies

Hong Kong, Singapore and the Greater Bay Area — choose the market where you want to build your career.

🎓

Led by practitioners

Admission

Entry requirements

A Bachelor's degree or the equivalent in biological/life sciences, chemistry, biomedical sciences/engineering, health science, or a related discipline.

  • Preference will be given to applicants having at least 1 year of relevant work experience.
  • If you are not a native speaker of English, and your Bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification is awarded by institutions where the medium of instruction is not English, you are expected to provide one of the following proficiency test results (taken in a single sitting within 2 years) to fulfil the University's minimum English language requirement for admission purposes:
    • An overall score of 80 out of 120 / 4.5 out of 6 (for tests taken on or after 21 Jan 2026) or above in the Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL) Internet-based test
    • OR
    • An Overall Band score of 6.0 or above in the International English Language Testing System (IELTS) Academic module

    Remarks: TOEFL iBT Home Edition, the IELTS Online, IELTS Indicator test, and IELTS One Skill Retake results will not be considered for meeting the English language requirement for taught programmes.

Please refer to the Admission Requirements section for details.
